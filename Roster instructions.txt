The following countries are rosterable:

NCR side:
NCR, Mojave Territories, Desert Rangers, New Vegas, New Canaan, Baggers

Legion side:
Caesar's Legion, Lanius' Cohort, Gente Del Sol, Two Sun, White Legs, The Eighties

Start with a roster of Caesar's Legion, NCR, Gente Del Sol, and Mojave Territories. For every 2 players you have after that roster one of the following pairs: 
White Legs & New Canaan, Baggers Expedition & Lanius's Cohort, Brotherhood of Steel & Desert Rangers, Desert Rangers & Eighties. 

If you have an odd number of players then in addition roster one of the following countries that hasn't been picked before: 
Desert Rangers, Brotherhood of Steel, Lanius's Cohort. 

Both New Vegas and Two Sun can be rostered at any player count without affecting the brigade balance of both sides. If you're rostering one of these two countries then don't count that player as part of the total for the previous instructions.
